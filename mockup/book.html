Meditations

Marcus Aurelius · c. 170–180 CE
12 books 14 ideas curated Greek original · Long 1862 translation Public domain

Private notebooks written in Greek by a Roman emperor on campaign. Aurelius never intended to publish them; they read as a mind steadying itself, each morning, against the shape of its own day. The through-line is a few repeated disciplines — of perception, of action, of will — returned to until they harden into habit.
