Atomic Habits

James Clear
  • Habits are the compound interest of self-improvement: 1% better each day is 37× better in a year.
  • You do not rise to the level of your goals — you fall to the level of your systems.
  • Identity-based change beats outcome-based change. Ask "what would a runner do?" not "how do I run more?"
  • The four laws: make it obvious, attractive, easy, satisfying. Reverse them to break a habit.
